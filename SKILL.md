## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one small folk totem, not a scene. Use a paper-cut silhouette, flat color, a few inner cuts and a slight overlap. Keep the awkwardness of a folk pattern saved for a long time. The mark stays small against a huge blank and may sit off center. Color is powder blue, ivory, plant green, coral, warm yellow or ochre, in a clear hierarchy taken from the photograph. Type is a tiny note beside a handmade seal. Reject a full scene, smooth vector, commercial cartoon, 3D and cheap souvenir styling.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-306/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
